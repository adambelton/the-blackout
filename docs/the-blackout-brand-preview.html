The
Blackout
A live literary broadcast platform for the 3pm football blackout.
Real football writers shape the story. AI orchestrates the telling.

Each weekend, a single match. A chosen narrative voice. Match events, club history, rivalry mythology and player storytelling woven together in real time — delivered as a live audiobook with AI-generated illustrations, shared simultaneously in a social room.

The game provides the structure.
The gaps provide the meaning.

Brand tone

Considered, not corporate. Every element earns its place.
Literary, not academic. Warm and human, not cold and institutional.
Quietly rebellious. Against the blackout, against generic sports design.
Japandi register. Warm neutrals, natural greens, generous negative space.

Notes for the designer

—Typeface is DM Sans (Google Fonts). Weights: 300 light, 400 regular, 500 medium.
—All colours defined in docs/BRAND.md in the project repository.
—Border radius: 10–12px for cards, 100px for pills. No shadows — depth via colour contrast only.
—The forest card (Ivory on Forest) is the hero treatment for featured contexts.
—Title splits "The" (small, driftwood) from "Blackout" (large, umber/ivory).
—The wordmark can be paired with the descriptive line “Live football fiction”.
