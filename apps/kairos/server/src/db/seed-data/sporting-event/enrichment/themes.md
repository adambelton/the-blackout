## Reading shape

For a sporting event, weight tracks how close a theme sits to the contest itself: a theme the result turns on — a side's season riding on the match, a figure the game is being decided around — runs high, while one that only colours the occasion stays low however vivid it is.

## Brief — extraction guidance

For a sporting event, the themes that tend to surface are recognisable shapes: redemption or return, the latest chapter of a rivalry, a reputation or a job under pressure, the weight of what is at stake, an individual's form set against the collective, an underdog defying the odds, the history between these sides. A live moment — a goal, a card, a swing of momentum — earns its meaning by the theme it advances; when one bears out a theme the brief named, that is the cue to weigh it up.

## Brief — initialisation guidance

For a sporting event, the themes a writer commits to track are usually set by the meeting itself — the stakes the table or the calendar has put on it, the storylines the two sides carry in, the figures the occasion has placed under the spotlight.
