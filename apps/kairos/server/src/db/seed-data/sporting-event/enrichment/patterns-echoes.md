## Reading shape

For a sporting event, a recurrence shows up as a shape that keeps returning — a set-piece routine that threatens each time, a player repeatedly at the heart of the danger, the same passage of play recurring, a scoreline pattern echoing an earlier match. `weight` rises when the recurrence bears on the contest's outcome rather than merely colouring it.

## Brief — extraction guidance

For a sporting event, the echoes a brief seeds are the histories it carries in — a head-to-head record, a player's history against this opponent, a manager's pattern in matches like this, a venue or fixture's recurring story, a comeback or collapse the side is known for. When a live event touches one of these, that is the echo; populate `echoesContextEntryIds` with the brief fragment it sounds against.
