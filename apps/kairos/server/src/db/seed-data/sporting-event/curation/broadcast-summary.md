## Task

For a sporting event, the through-line is the state of the contest — who is ahead and how, what just shifted, what is at stake in the next passage — carried in the broadcast's voice. Never a scoreline readout or a stats list; the single statement a narrator would want at the top of the prompt.

## Brief — extraction guidance

For a sporting event, the through-lines a brief carries are the stakes, the storylines the two sides bring, and the figures to follow; let them shape which thread ties the cycle together.
