## Task

For a sporting event, the same broad point recurs as "the same side still pressing", "still camped in the other half", "still chasing the equaliser" — phrased fresh each cycle but advancing nothing. A goal, a card, or a clear shift in the game is what makes a subject genuinely fresh again; restating sustained pressure with no change underneath is the textbook saturation case.

## Brief — extraction guidance

For a sporting event, the subjects a brief flags as central — the headline storylines, the figures the broadcast follows — earn more saturation tolerance because they are meant to recur as carrying threads. Peripheral colour saturates fast.
