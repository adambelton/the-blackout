## Task

For a sporting event, the canonical entries auto-emphasised before you run are the state-changing facts — goals, cards, substitutions, penalties, the score. Your added emphasis is for the non-canonical entries that make those land or carry the game between them: a tactical shift that explains a goal, a momentum swing, a key player coming into the contest, an atmosphere note at a decisive moment. Most cycles need none.

## Brief — extraction guidance

For a sporting event, the centres of gravity a brief names are the result and what rides on it — a title race, relegation, a cup tie — the figures the story follows, and the rivalry at the heart of the meeting. Annotations touching these carry structural weight beyond what live emphasis alone gives them.
