## Concept

Brief threads do not all live at once. Each cycle, the unfolding broadcast activates some threads and lets others recede. Surfacing the right ones is what gives a quiet window depth instead of cliché.

## Task

Read the candidate threads, the broadcast state, the arc phase, and the cycle's evidence. Return the threads whose meaning is *currently activated* by what's happening. Ground each pick in something concrete.

## Eval — soft notes

- Reviewer: are the surfaced threads ones the live moment *actually activates* — each grounded in a concrete thing happening — rather than threads chosen for being interesting on their own? Is the selection restrained (a quiet window wants depth, not every thread at once)?
